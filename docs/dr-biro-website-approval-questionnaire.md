# Dr. Biro website approval questionnaire

This document collects the remaining facts and approvals needed to improve
patient guidance without guessing about office policies or clinical claims.
Please answer only for services and locations where the information is current.

## Practice positioning

1. Is “Serving patients in the greater Los Angeles area” accurate for all four
   listed locations? If not, what wording should the website use?
2. Should the website continue to lead with “Nicolas Biro, M.D.” while using the
   new symbol without the proposed practice name?
3. Are consultations currently available in English, Spanish, and French at all
   four locations?

## Appointments and payment

For each office, please confirm:

- consultation fee, or whether the fee varies by concern;
- insurance plans accepted and whether participation differs by service;
- referral or prior-authorization requirements;
- typical consultation duration;
- whether dilation or other testing may occur;
- when a driver may be needed;
- cosmetic payment or financing options;
- current office hours and scheduling contact;
- cancellation or rescheduling policy; and
- postoperative and after-hours contact process.

Please identify any information that changes too often to publish safely. The
website can instead tell patients exactly what to ask the scheduling team.

## Medical content approval

1. May procedure and symptom pages display “Medically reviewed by Dr. Nicolas
   Biro” after you review them?
2. What review date should be displayed for the current content?
3. May the website list you as the author, medical reviewer, or both?
4. Are the current urgent-warning statements approved, particularly the sudden
   eyelid drooping guidance?
5. Are there preferred clinical sources or patient handouts that should replace
   or supplement the current citations?

## Gallery authorization

For every case intended to remain public, please confirm:

- written authorization for website publication;
- approved procedure name and description;
- approved before-and-after ordering and view labels;
- approximate time between treatment and the after photograph, if available;
- whether the case should carry a sensitive-content warning; and
- whether the case may appear only in the gallery or also on a procedure page.

## Referrals and operations

1. Which office should referring clinicians contact for each major condition?
2. What secure method should clinicians use to send records and imaging?
3. What documentation should accompany a routine referral?
4. What is the approved urgent-referral pathway?
5. Are there conditions or patient ages that should not be represented as part of
   the practice scope?

## Reputation and external profiles

1. Are the linked Healthgrades, WebMD, Pacific Eye Institute, DLV Vision, Laser
   Eye Center, A Center for Vision Care, and LinkedIn profiles current?
2. Is the Zocdoc profile current and approved for identity linking?
3. May the website link to additional independent review profiles?
4. Which portrait and biography should external practices use for consistency?

## Accessibility, language, and video

1. Should professionally reviewed Spanish pages be commissioned now? If so,
   which pages should be translated first?
2. Who will clinically review translated medical content?
3. Is Dr. Biro willing to record short educational videos, and which topics are
   approved?
4. Are there office-specific accessibility accommodations patients should request
   before arriving?

## Measurement and privacy

1. May the website use aggregate, cookieless measurement for appointment, phone,
   direction, and office-selection clicks?
2. Who should receive a monthly summary of those counts?
3. Is a privacy-policy update approved if measurement is introduced?

No symptoms, medical information, form content, insurance details, or patient
identifiers should be collected through website measurement.
